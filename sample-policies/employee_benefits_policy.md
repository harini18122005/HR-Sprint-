## 1. Overview and Philosophy
Acme Global Technologies offers a competitive, comprehensive benefits package designed to safeguard employee health, provide financial resilience, and foster continuous personal and professional development.

## 2. Health, Dental, and Vision Coverage
### 2.1 Enrollment and Eligibility
Coverage begins on the first calendar day of the month following the employee's official start date. Employees may enroll themselves and eligible dependents (spouse, domestic partner, and dependent children up to age 26).
- **Open Enrollment**: The annual open enrollment window occurs every November for coverage effective January 1.
- **Qualifying Life Events**: Changes outside open enrollment (e.g., marriage, birth of child, loss of alternate coverage) must be submitted within 30 days of the qualifying event.

### 2.2 Plan Options
- **Preferred Provider Organization (PPO)**: Higher flexibility, Acme covers 85% of employee-only premiums and 70% of dependent premiums.
- **High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA**: Paired with an employer-funded Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution of $1,000 annually for individuals or $2,000 for families. Acme covers 95% of employee-only premiums.
- **Dental & Vision**: Standard preventive cleanings and comprehensive annual vision examinations are covered at 100% in-network.

## 3. Retirement and 401(k) Savings Plan
- **Employer Match**: Acme Global matches 100% of employee contributions up to the first 4% of eligible base salary, and 50% on the next 2% (maximum total company match of 5%).
- **Vesting Schedule**: Employer matching contributions vest immediately upon deposit (100% immediate vesting).
- **Enrollment**: Automatic enrollment at 4% occurs 30 days after hire date unless an alternative election or opt-out is submitted.

## 4. Wellness and Home Office Stipends
- **Monthly Wellness Allowance**: All active full-time staff receive a tax-advantaged $75 monthly wellness stipend applicable to gym memberships, mental health apps, ergonomic accessories, or fitness equipment.
- **Remote Work Setup Grant**: New remote and hybrid hires receive a one-time $500 home office reimbursement within their first 90 days of employment for monitors, desks, or ergonomic seating.

## 5. Professional Growth and Tuition Assistance
- **Continuing Education**: Full-time employees who have completed 1 year of continuous service are eligible for up to $3,000 per calendar year in tuition or professional certification reimbursement.
- **Course Pre-approval**: Courses and certifications must directly align with current responsibilities or established career progression pathways at Acme Global and require manager sign-off prior to course registration.
