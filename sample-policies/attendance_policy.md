## 1. Statement of Principle
Consistent attendance and punctuality are vital to maintaining operational excellence, team trust, and collaborative momentum. Acme Global Technologies respects work-life harmony while maintaining structured expectations for availability and accountability.

## 2. Standard Working Hours and Core Collaboration Window
### 2.1 Standard Schedule
The standard full-time workweek comprises 40 hours, typically scheduled Monday through Friday. Standard office hours are 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM in the employee's designated local time zone, with an unpaid 1-hour lunch break.

### 2.2 Core Collaboration Hours
To facilitate synchronous cross-functional collaboration, all salaried personnel (both in-office and remote) are expected to be online and available during **Core Hours**:
- **Core Hours**: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time, Monday through Thursday.
- During core hours, employees must be reachable via company messaging tools (Slack/Teams) and accessible for scheduled team meetings.

## 3. Remote and Hybrid Attendance Tracking
- **Hybrid Schedules**: Employees designated as hybrid are expected to work from an authorized Acme office at least 2 days per week (typically Tuesday and Thursday, unless departmentally modified).
- **Virtual Check-in**: Remote and hybrid employees record daily presence by clocking in on the People Portal or updating their status indicator upon commencing their workday.
- **Off-Site Working**: Temporary remote work from alternative geographic locations is permitted for up to 15 business days per year, provided prior written consent is obtained from the department head.

## 4. Punctuality, Tardiness, and Unplanned Absences
### 4.1 Notice of Absence or Late Arrival
If an employee anticipates being late by more than 15 minutes or cannot attend work due to emergency or sudden illness, they must notify their direct manager via company email or messaging by 8:30 AM local time or at least 30 minutes before their scheduled start time.

### 4.2 Excessive Tardiness and Unexcused Absence
- **Excessive Tardiness**: Arriving late or failing to attend core hours three or more times in a 30-day period without managerial pre-approval constitutes excessive tardiness.
- **Unexcused Absence (No Call, No Show)**: Failure to report to work and failure to notify management for two consecutive working days will be treated as job abandonment and voluntary resignation.

## 5. Overtime and Compensatory Time
- **Non-Exempt Employees**: Overtime work (exceeding 40 hours per workweek) must be pre-authorized in writing by an authorized manager. Overtime is compensated at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.
- **Exempt Employees**: Salaried exempt employees are compensated on an annual salary basis and do not qualify for overtime pay, but may take informal flex hours with managerial approval during high-volume deliverables.
