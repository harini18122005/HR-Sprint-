## 1. Purpose and Scope
Acme Global Technologies believes that personal time off is vital for employee health, well-being, and sustained productivity. This policy defines the guidelines, accrual rates, and approval procedures for Paid Time Off (PTO), Sick Leave, Parental Leave, and Special Leave.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
### 2.1 Accrual Rates
Full-time regular employees accrue PTO on a semi-monthly basis according to their completed years of continuous service:
- **0 to 2 Years of Service**: 15 days (120 hours) per calendar year, accrued at 5.0 hours per pay period.
- **3 to 5 Years of Service**: 20 days (160 hours) per calendar year, accrued at 6.67 hours per pay period.
- **6+ Years of Service**: 25 days (200 hours) per calendar year, accrued at 8.33 hours per pay period.

Part-time employees working 20 hours or more per week accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis proportional to their standard scheduled hours.

### 2.2 Maximum Rollover and Forfeiture
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days (40 hours) into the following calendar year. Any unused PTO exceeding 40 hours on December 31 will be automatically forfeited unless an exception is approved in writing by the Chief People Officer.

## 3. Sick Leave and Wellness Days
All full-time employees receive 10 dedicated sick leave days (80 hours) granted on January 1 of each calendar year. Sick leave is designated for:
- Personal illness, injury, or medical appointments.
- Caring for an immediate family member who is ill or receiving medical care.
- Mental health and preventative wellness recovery.

Sick days do not carry over to subsequent calendar years and are not payable upon departure from the company. A physician's note is required only for absences extending beyond 3 consecutive working days.

## 4. Parental Leave
Acme Global provides 16 weeks of fully paid Parental Leave to eligible employees following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Eligibility**: Employees must have completed at least 6 months of continuous full-time service prior to the qualifying event.
- **Flexibility**: Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival and may be utilized in up to two distinct blocks with managerial agreement.

## 5. Bereavement and Compassionate Leave
Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave in the event of the loss of a family member:
- **Immediate Family (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling)**: Up to 5 consecutive paid working days.
- **Extended Family (Grandparent, In-law, Aunt/Uncle)**: Up to 3 consecutive paid working days.

Additional unpaid leave or PTO substitution may be requested through People Operations.

## 6. Request and Approval Procedure
1. **Advance Notice**: Scheduled PTO requests exceeding 2 consecutive working days must be submitted through the HR Portal at least 2 weeks in advance.
2. **Approval**: Direct supervisors review requests based on business coverage, project deadlines, and team capacity.
3. **Emergency Absence**: In case of unanticipated illness or emergency, employees must notify their manager and log the absence by 9:00 AM local time on the day of absence.
